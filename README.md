**VICE COVER STUDIO** turns your own photos into GTA 6-style multi-panel cover art — the iconic poster look with a Vice City neon-sunset grade — and exports it as a PNG ready to post. Pick from 6 layouts (four-panel grid, hero + three, full-bleed, nine-panel grid, tilted snapshot collage, story layout), grade every panel with 6 gradient-map looks (sunset, neon pink/teal, golden hour, midnight purple, vintage, Miami pastel), crop and position each panel, write a big display caption with subtitle and badge ribbon, then export one-click PNGs in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080 — or all three at once. Everything runs offline in the browser: photos never leave your machine, and two sample photos are bundled so you can try it instantly. Undo/redo, live preview, film grain, vignette and a halftone title block included. An original design — no Rockstar logos, artwork or fonts; all type is SIL Open Font License (Anton, Bebas Neue, Pacifico, Oswald, shipped under `app/fonts/`).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
